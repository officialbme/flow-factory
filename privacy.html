Privacy Policy

How Flow Factory collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Flow Factory ("Flow Factory," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you contact us, request an estimate, receive our services, or communicate with us by phone, email, or text message. Flow Factory is a gutter and permanent exterior lighting company based in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma. By using our website or services, you agree to this Policy.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as your name, home or service address, phone number, email address, and details about the work you are requesting. We collect this when you fill out our estimate form, call or text us, or schedule and receive service. We may also collect basic, non-identifying usage data from our website (such as pages visited) to help us improve it.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Schedule, perform, and follow up on the gutter and lighting work you request;
  • Prepare and send estimates, invoices, and receipts;
  • Communicate with you about your job, including appointment and service updates; and
  • With your consent, send you a text message asking you to leave a review of our work.

SMS / text messaging

If you provide your phone number and consent, Flow Factory may send you SMS text messages related to your service and, with your permission, a request to leave an online review. Consent to receive texts is obtained when you provide your mobile number for this purpose or verbally agree during a call. Message types may include service and appointment updates and review requests.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message, and you can reply HELP for assistance or contact us at 405-802-8704. See our Terms & Conditions for full SMS terms.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your mobile opt-in and phone number are never shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to run our business — for example, with service providers who help us operate, such as our text-messaging provider or payment processor — and only so they can perform services on our behalf. We may also disclose information if required by law. These providers are not permitted to use your information for their own marketing.

Data retention & security

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal, tax, and accounting requirements, then dispose of it securely. We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask us to update or delete your information, and you may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. To make a request, contact us using the details below.
