Every December it's the same ladder, the same tangled strands, the same cold afternoon taking them back down. Here's an honest look at how permanent exterior lighting compares to hanging your own — on cost, safety, and whether it pays off.

The real cost

What hanging lights actually costs you

The sticker price on a box of lights is the smallest part of the bill. Every year you're paying for new strands to replace the ones that half-lit in storage, new clips, and the one thing you can't buy back — your time, up a ladder, in Oklahoma cold, twice. Hire it out and a professional hang-and-remove runs hundreds of dollars per season, forever.

Permanent lighting flips that math. You pay once for a system that's built into the house and rated to last for years. If you decorate every December — or you've been meaning to and never get around to it — it typically pays for itself within a few seasons, and everything after that is free. No ladder, no strands, no "we'll do it next weekend."

Beyond the holidays

One system, used all year

Hanging lights do one job for three weeks. Permanent lighting earns its keep the other eleven months, too:

  • Everyday curb appeal. Soft warm-white accent lighting that makes the house look finished every single night.
  • Game day & every holiday. OU crimson, Thunder blue, orange for Halloween, red-white-and-blue for the Fourth — set from your phone in seconds.
  • Security & schedules. Program it to come on at dusk and off at bedtime — a lit house, automatically, whether you're home or not.
  • Set it and forget it. Schedule the whole year once and let it run — no app-fiddling required unless you want to.

How it goes in

Built into the house, not clipped on

We mount a low-profile LED channel up under your soffit and color-match it to your trim, so by day it reads as a thin line and all but disappears. Inside the channel sit individually addressable LEDs — that's what lets you run any color, pattern, or schedule from an app. The system is UL-certified, IP68 waterproof, and Dark-Sky friendly, rated for 100,000+ hours. Most homes are done in a day, and we leave it tidy.

Good to know

Common questions

Are permanent lights worth it compared to hanging Christmas lights?

For most homeowners, yes. You pay once instead of every season, you never climb a ladder or pay a crew to hang and remove strands, and you get year-round use — warm white every day, team colors on game day, and a full holiday show in December. If you decorate every year, permanent lighting usually pays for itself within a few seasons.

Do permanent exterior lights stay up all year?

Yes. The LED track is mounted once under your soffit and stays there. It's color-matched to your trim so it's nearly invisible by day, and you turn it on only when you want it — holidays, game nights, everyday accent light, or security.

Do permanent lights look bad during the daytime?

No. Unlike clipped-on Christmas strands and visible cords, the track tucks up under the soffit and is matched to your trim color, so it reads as a thin line and all but disappears from the ground.

Can I change the colors?

Yes. Every point is an individually addressable LED, so you can set millions of colors, warm whites, patterns, and schedules right from an app — OU crimson, Thunder blue, orange for Halloween, red and green for Christmas, or soft white year-round.

How long does permanent lighting last?

The LEDs are rated for 100,000+ hours and the system is UL-certified and IP68 waterproof — built to stay on the house and keep working for years, through Oklahoma summers and ice storms alike.

Do permanent lights use a lot of electricity?

No. They're low-draw LEDs, so running them costs only pennies a night — far less than older incandescent holiday strands.
