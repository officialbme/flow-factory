Permanent Exterior LED Lighting

Discreet LED track tucked under your eaves — invisible by day, stunning by night. Any color, any schedule, run from your phone. Never hang a staple-gun light again.

Permanent exterior lighting is a low-profile LED track mounted up under your soffit. By day it disappears into the trim; by night it is warm-white curb appeal, team colors for game day, or red-and-green for the holidays — all from an app, all on a schedule you set once. No tangled strands, no ladders in December, no lights that quit after one season.

Each point is an individually addressable LED, so you are not stuck with one color. Set warm white for everyday, program OU crimson or Thunder blue for game nights, orange for Halloween, and a full holiday show for December — then let it run itself. It is built in, not clipped on, and rated to stay up for years.

What you get

Done the right way

  • Any color, any night. Millions of colors plus tunable warm whites, controlled right from your phone.
  • Set it and forget it. Schedule holidays, game days, and everyday accent lighting — it runs on its own.
  • Built in, not clipped on. Low-profile track mounts under the soffit or fascia — bare by day, brilliant at night.
  • Pro-grade & weatherproof. UL-certified, IP68 waterproof, Dark-Sky friendly, rated 100,000+ hours.
  • Color-matched track. The channel is matched to your trim so the hardware stays invisible in daylight.
  • One crew, installed clean. The same team that does your gutters wires and mounts the lighting — tidy and to code.

Good to know

Common questions

How much does permanent lighting cost?

Pricing is based on the linear footage of roofline you want lit and the complexity of the run. We measure in person and give itemized pricing — call 405-802-8704 for a free estimate.

Can I see the lights during the day?

The track is low-profile and color-matched to your trim, so it reads as a thin line and all but disappears from the ground. At night it is the star of the block.

Do I control it from my phone?

Yes — colors, brightness, patterns, and schedules all run from an app. Set it once for the year or change it any night you like.

How long does the lighting last?

The LEDs are rated for 100,000+ hours and the system is UL-certified and IP68 waterproof — it is built to stay on the house and keep working for years.
