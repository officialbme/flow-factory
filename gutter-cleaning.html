Gutter Cleaning & Maintenance

Clear gutters and downspouts before storm season — we flush the system, test the flow, and bag the debris. Small problems caught before they get expensive.

Clogged gutters are where water damage starts. When leaves and grit pack a run solid, the next hard rain pours over the edge — soaking fascia, splashing siding, and pooling against the foundation. A seasonal clean-out keeps water moving through the system the way it was built to.

We clear every run and downspout by hand, flush the drops to confirm they actually flow, and bag the debris off your property. While we are up there we eye the hangers, seams, and pitch — so if something is starting to sag or leak, you hear about it before it turns into a repair.

What you get

Done the right way

  • Full clear-out. Every gutter run and downspout cleared by hand — not just the easy stretches.
  • Flow-tested drops. We flush each downspout to confirm water runs all the way to the ground.
  • Debris hauled away. Bagged and off your property — no pile left in the flower bed.
  • Free health check. We flag loose hangers, failed seams, and bad pitch while we are up there.
  • Before storm season. Spring and fall are the smart times — clear before the heavy rain, not after.
  • Guards available. If you are tired of the cycle, ask about leaf filter guards to cut it way down.

Good to know

Common questions

How often should gutters be cleaned?

Most OKC-metro homes do well with twice a year — late spring and late fall. Heavy tree cover (oaks, cottonwoods, pines) can call for more. Gutter guards stretch the interval way out.

How much does gutter cleaning cost?

It depends on the size of the home, the number of stories, and how packed the gutters are. We give a straight quote up front — call 405-802-8704.

Do you repair what you find?

Yes. If the clean-out turns up a sag, leak, or bad slope, we can re-hang and reseal it — often on the same visit. No surprise charges without your okay first.
